Guests are added automatically when they book. Open **Customers** to search by name, email or phone.

The list shows **Visits**, **No-shows**, **Total Spent** and the first and last visit. Regular guests get a **Regular** badge.

Tap a customer to see **Customer Info**, **Bookings**, **Fees** and **Notes**. Notes are for your team only.

Coming from another tool? Import your customers under **Settings → Import**.
