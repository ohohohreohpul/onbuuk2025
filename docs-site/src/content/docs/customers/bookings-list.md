Open **Bookings**. Filter by **Needs action**, **Upcoming**, **Past**, **Cancelled & no-shows** or **All**, or search by name or email.

The column **Next step** tells you what is still open: confirm, "Did they show up?", payment. Tap **Open** to do it; the steps are the same as in the calendar, see [Confirm, take payment, move or cancel](/pos/manage-booking/).

New appointments are added in [POS](/pos/create-booking/).
