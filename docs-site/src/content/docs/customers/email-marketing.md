---
title: Email marketing
description: Send your customers a promotion once a month.
sidebar:
  order: 4
---

1. Open **Email marketing** and tap **New promotion**.
2. Write the **Subject** and the **Text**. `{vorname}` becomes the customer's first name.
3. Optionally add a button with a link, for example to your voucher page.
4. **Who receives it?** **Newsletter subscribers only**, or **All customers with email** (only those who agreed).
5. Choose the day of the month and the time, and whether it is sent **automatically every month**.
6. **Send a test** to yourself first, then **Send now** or let it go out on its day.

Every email contains your address and an unsubscribe link. Guests who unsubscribe still get their appointment and voucher emails.
