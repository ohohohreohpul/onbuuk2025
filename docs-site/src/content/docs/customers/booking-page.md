Guests book in a few steps:

1. **Welcome**: *Book an Appointment* or *Purchase a Gift Card*.
2. **Choose Your Service** (for couples treatments: individual or couples session).
3. **Select Duration**.
4. **Add-On Products**, if the treatment has extras.
5. **Choose Your Specialist**, only if guests may choose (see [Specialists](/settings/specialists/)).
6. **Choose Date & Time**: only real free times are shown.
7. **Your Details**: name, email, phone, notes.
8. **Review & Pay**: pay by card, PayPal, or in person, depending on your [payment settings](/money/payments/). Gift cards and passes can be used here.

Afterwards the guest sees **Booking confirmed**, or **Booking request received** if you confirm bookings yourself. Every email has a link to cancel.

Guests can create an account to see their appointments, their gift cards and passes, and their reward points.

## Change the look

In **Settings → Booking Form** you change the texts of each step, the pictures and the layout. Colours are in **Settings → Colors**. To put the booking on your own website, see **Settings → Widget & Embed**.
