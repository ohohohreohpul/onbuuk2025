Open **Tax & Reports**:

- **Records**: your cash book (Kassenbuch). Counter sales and voucher sales appear here. **Add record** for anything else. A cancelled entry gets a "Storno" line; nothing is deleted.
- **Booking payments**: payments by month, with **Export CSV**.
- **Reports & Documents**: EÜR summary, DATEV export, default tax rates and accounts.
- **Import**: bring in records from Treatwell, Planity or any CSV.

Invoice details (your legal name, tax number, VAT rate, Kleinunternehmer) are set in **Settings → Invoices**.
