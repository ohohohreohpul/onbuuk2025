---
title: No-show fees
description: Charge, collect or waive the fee when a guest does not come.
sidebar:
  order: 3
---

**Set the fee** per treatment in **Services**: **No-show fee**, **Late cancellation fee** and **Cancel before (hours)**.

**When a guest does not come**, mark the booking **No, did not come**. If the guest left a card when booking, Zenno tries to charge the fee. Otherwise it waits in **No-Show Fees**.

In **No-Show Fees** you see **Outstanding**, **Collected** and **Waived**, and for each fee you can:

- **Charge card**: charge the saved card,
- **Mark paid**: the guest paid another way,
- **Waive**: let it go,
- **Reopen**: undo.

Below you see how often guests do not come, on which weekdays, and who does it repeatedly. Guests who missed several times must pay in advance when they book online.
