---
title: Sell a voucher at the counter
description: Sell a voucher to a guest standing in front of you, in a few taps.
sidebar:
  order: 1
---

1. In **POS**, tap **Sell voucher**.
2. **What is sold?** Choose a **Value voucher** (or type an **Other amount**), one of your **Voucher sets**, or a **Multi-visit card**.
3. **How was it paid?** Tap **Cash** or **Card**.
4. Optional: the **Buyer**'s name and email.
5. Tap **Sold – issue voucher**.

The voucher is paid and active straight away. It appears in your cash book and in the voucher list. Tap **Print / download PDF** to hand it over, or **Sell another**.
