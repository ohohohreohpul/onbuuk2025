Open **Vouchers**. The tabs:

- **Gift cards**: every card and pass. **Issue a card or pass** (a **Value card** or a **Service pass**), download the PDF, email it to the recipient, or import cards from another system (**Import CSV**).
- **Gift-card setup**: suggested values, a custom amount with minimum and maximum, expiry and code format. **Service passes** (for example "5 × Thai massage 60 min"): **Create a service pass**. **PDF design**: your artwork and terms next to the QR code.
- **Orders & sets**: voucher sets and online orders that are **paid in the shop**. When the guest pays, tap **Paid cash** or **Paid by card**, print the vouchers and tick when handed over. You can also run a promotion with a gift above a minimum amount.
- **Point of sale**: redeem a card or pass by code, and see pending payments.
- **Loyalty program**: points per amount spent, points for a reward, a welcome bonus and expiry.

To sell or redeem at the counter, the quickest way is the calendar: [Sell a voucher](/money/sell-voucher/) and [Redeem a voucher](/money/redeem-voucher/).
