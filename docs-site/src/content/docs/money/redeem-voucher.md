---
title: Redeem a voucher
description: A guest pays with a voucher. Check the code and take off the amount.
sidebar:
  order: 2
---

**When the guest pays for a booking:** open the booking, go to **3 · Payment**, tap **Gift card**, enter the code and tap **Redeem**.

**Without a booking:** in **POS**, tap **Redeem voucher**.

1. Scan the **QR code** on the voucher with the camera, or type the **code**.
2. Zenno shows the remaining value (or the visits left on a multi-visit card).
3. Enter the amount to take off and confirm.

The rest stays on the voucher for the next visit.
