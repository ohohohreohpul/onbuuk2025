title: Online payments
description: Accept card payments, PayPal or payment at the shop.

  order: 5
---

Only the shop owner can change payments. Go to **Settings → Payment**.

- **Card payments (Stripe):** tap **Connect Stripe**, choose your country and finish the setup with Stripe. When **Charges enabled** and **Payouts enabled** show, switch on **Accept online card payments**.
- **Pay at venue:** switch on **Show 'Pay at venue' at checkout**. For treatments with a no-show fee, the guest leaves a card as a guarantee and is not charged now.
- **PayPal:** switch on **Accept PayPal** and enter your PayPal client ID and secret.

Payments at the counter are recorded on the booking: see [Confirm, take payment, move or cancel](/pos/manage-booking/).
