Open the tab **Personality**. On the right, **How it sounds** shows a sample reply that changes as you choose.

## Learn from your website

1. Tap **Learn from my website**.
2. Enter your website address, for example `www.my-spa.de`, and tap **Read website**.
3. Zenno reads your homepage and your "about us" page and fills in personality, length, emojis and a short briefing in your shop's voice.
4. Check everything and tap **Save**. Nothing changes until you save.

Prices, opening hours and offers are never taken from the website. Those always come from your app.

## Set it yourself

- **Personality**: Warm & caring, Calm & elegant, Cheerful & upbeat, or Professional.
- **How much she says**: Short, Balanced or Detailed.
- **Emojis**: Never, Like the guest, or Now and then.
- **How proactive**: Just answer, Guide to booking, or Sell actively (recommends treatments, mentions vouchers and offers).
- **Good habits**: for example introduce herself in the first message, ask what the guest needs first, use the guest's first name, suggest a voucher for birthdays, offer other times when full, add a caring tip after booking.
- **Your briefing**: in your own words, what your shop feels like and how guests should be treated. For example: "We are a small family-run Thai spa. Treat everyone like a guest in our home. Many guests come with back pain, so ask how they feel."

Tap **Save**.

## Name and form of address

In **What it knows** → **Name and form of address** you can give the receptionist a name (for example "Mali") and choose **Sie** or **du** for German. Without a name it speaks as your shop: "Hi, this is SATUUU99".
