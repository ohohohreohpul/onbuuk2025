The tab **What it knows** shows everything the receptionist uses, straight from your app: treatments and prices, therapists, opening hours, vouchers, the current promotion and your booking rules. Nothing here can contradict your app.

To change something, tap **Change** next to it. You go to the place where it is set up, for example **Services** for prices.

## Questions and answers

For things the app does not know (parking, directions, what to bring), add short entries under **Questions and answers**: one question, one short answer.

## Therapist names

If guests cannot choose their therapist on your booking page, the receptionist does not name therapists either. It only offers times.
