In the tab **WhatsApp messages** you send a message to many customers at once, for example to announce free times or a voucher offer. Replies land in your [Inbox](/ai-receptionist/inbox/), and the receptionist answers them.

## Only with consent

You may only write to customers who **agreed** to get WhatsApp messages from you. Under **Import a customer list with consent**, tap **Choose CSV file**: a list with the columns name, phone, email and consent (yes/no). Only rows with consent = yes get messages. Every message says how to stop, and anyone who replies **STOP** is taken off the list automatically.

## Send

1. Write a **Name (only for you)** and the **Message**. `{name}` becomes the customer's first name.
2. Check how many customers it goes to.
3. Tap **Send** and confirm. You can **Pause** and **Continue** at any time.

Messages go out slowly, a few at a time, to keep your number safe.
