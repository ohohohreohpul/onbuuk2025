The **Inbox** tab works like a messenger: all chats on the left, the open chat on the right. On a phone you see the list first, then the chat.

## Find a chat

- **Search chats**: by name, phone, email or text.
- Filters: **All**, **Needs you**, and the channel icons for WhatsApp, email and website.
- A small badge on the avatar shows the channel. A green dot means unread. **Needs you** marks chats where the AI handed over or a draft waits for you.

## Reply

Open a chat, write in the box at the bottom and press **Enter** (Shift + Enter for a new line). Your reply goes out on the same channel: WhatsApp, email or the website chat.

When you reply yourself, you can switch the AI off for that one chat (**Team answers here**) and back on later (**AI answers here**).

## Drafts

If a channel is set to **Write drafts only**, the AI's reply waits in the chat as **Draft – not sent yet**. Tap **Send this reply**, **Edit** it first, or discard it.

## Start a new chat

Tap the pen button next to the search. Choose **WhatsApp** or **Email**, pick a customer or type a number or address, write your message and send.

:::note
New WhatsApp chats need the WhatsApp connection with your own number (QR code). Email needs your mailbox connected.
:::
