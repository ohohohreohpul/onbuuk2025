Open **AI receptionist** in the menu.

## 1. The main switch

At the top right you see the status: **On – the receptionist answers** or **Off – nobody is answered automatically**. Tap the switch to turn it on or off for all channels at once.

## 2. Connect channels

Open the tab **Channels**. At the top choose how it works for all channels: **Answer automatically** or **Write drafts only**.

### Website chat

1. Switch **Website chat** on.
2. Copy the line under **Code for your website** and put it into your website, just before `</body>` (or send it to whoever looks after your website).
3. A chat bubble appears on your website, in your colour and with your welcome text.

### Email

The receptionist reads new emails in your own mailbox and answers from it.

1. First set up your mailbox under **Settings → Emails** (see [Emails](/settings/emails/)).
2. In **Channels**, switch **Email** on and enter the **IMAP server** and **Port** of your mailbox. **User name** and **Password** can stay empty if they are the same as for sending.
3. Tap **Test and connect**. When it says **Connected**, new emails are answered.

### WhatsApp

1. Switch **WhatsApp** on and choose **Your own number (scan QR)**. (Larger shops can use **Official WhatsApp Business** instead.)
2. Tap **Connect**. A QR code appears.
3. On the shop phone open WhatsApp → **Linked devices** → **Link a device** and scan the code.
4. When it says **Connected** with your number, guests who write to you get answers.

Your phone keeps working as usual. Messages you send yourself from the phone are never answered by the AI.

## 3. Test it

- **Website:** in **Personality**, tap **Try it in the chat**.
- **WhatsApp without a second phone:** see [Test on WhatsApp](/ai-receptionist/test/).
