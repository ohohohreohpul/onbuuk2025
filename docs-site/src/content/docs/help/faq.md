### A guest cannot find a free time online.
Free times come from the therapists' **Working Hours**, minus bookings, blocked time and shop closed days. Check [Specialists](/settings/specialists/).

### Guests do not get emails.
Set up a sender in **Settings → Emails** and tap **Send Test Email**. See [Emails](/settings/emails/).

### The AI did not answer my WhatsApp test.
Messages from the shop's own phone are ignored on purpose. Start your test with `#test`. See [Test on WhatsApp](/ai-receptionist/test/).

### Can the AI give wrong prices?
It only uses prices from your app. If something is not in the app, it hands the chat to you.

### Someone on my team should not see money.
Give them the role **Staff** or **Receptionist**. See [Team and permissions](/settings/team/).

### I need help from a person.
Write to **support@zennohq.com**. See [Get help](/help/get-help/).
