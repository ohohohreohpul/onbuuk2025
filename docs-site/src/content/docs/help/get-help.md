In the app, tap **Get Help** at the bottom of the menu, then **Write email**. Your email app opens with your account details already filled in, so we can help faster.

Or write directly to **support@zennohq.com**.

Please tell us:

1. What you wanted to do.
2. What happened instead.
3. A screenshot, if you can.

A real person from the Zenno team answers.
