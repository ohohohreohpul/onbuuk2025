---
title: Add an appointment
description: Put in a booking for a guest who calls or walks in.
sidebar:
  order: 2
---

1. In **POS**, tap the free time where the appointment should start (in Day view: in the column of the therapist).
2. Choose **Add appointment**. The window **New appointment** opens.
3. Choose the **Treatment** and the **Duration**. The price is shown next to each duration.
4. Check the **Therapist** and **Day and time**. Zenno shows the **Free times**; tap one.
5. Under **Customer**, search by name, phone or email. Is the guest new? Tap **New customer** and enter **Name**, **Phone** and, if you have it, **Email** (the confirmation is sent there).
6. If needed, write a **Note for the team**, for example "strong pressure, allergies". Only your team sees it.
7. Tap **Add appointment**.

The appointment appears in the calendar straight away.

:::note[Searching by phone]
You can type the phone number any way you like: with spaces, with +49 or with 0. Zenno finds the guest by the last digits.
:::

:::tip[A caller wants to think about it]
Hold the time without making a booking: [block the time](/pos/block-time/) with a comment. Later you turn it into a real appointment with one tap.
:::
