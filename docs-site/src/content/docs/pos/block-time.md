Block time for a break, a holiday, a doctor's appointment, when a therapist has to leave, or to hold a time for someone on the phone. Guests cannot book blocked time online, and the AI receptionist does not offer it.

## Block a time

1. In **POS**, tap the time where the block should start and choose **Block time** (or use the **Block time** button at the top).
2. **Who?** Pick the therapist, or **Whole shop** if the shop is closed.
3. Set the **Date**, **From** and **Until**, or tick **Whole day**.
4. Pick a **Reason**: Break, Holiday, Sick, Private appointment or Closed.
5. Write a **Comment** if it helps, for example *Ms Weber · back & neck · 0151 2345 6789*.
6. Pick a **Colour** so you recognise it at a glance, for example green for phone reservations.
7. For the same time on more days, tick **Several days** and choose the **Last day**.
8. Tap **Save**.

The time shows as a striped field in your colour, with the comment inside.

## Turn a blocked time into an appointment

When the caller confirms:

1. Tap the blocked time.
2. Tap **Turn into a real appointment**.
3. The **New appointment** window opens with the time, therapist and the phone number from your comment already filled in. Check it and tap **Add appointment**.

The blocked time is removed only after the appointment is saved, so you never lose the time.

## Change or remove

Tap the blocked time, change what you need and **Save**, or tap **Remove block** and **Yes, remove**.
