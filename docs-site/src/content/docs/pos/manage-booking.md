Tap a booking in the calendar. The booking opens with three simple steps, plus the guest's details. Tap the phone number to call the guest.

## 1 · Confirmation

If the booking waits for you (yellow card, "Please confirm"), tap **Confirm booking** or **Decline**. When you decline, the guest is informed by email.

## 2 · Did the customer come?

On the day of the appointment, tap **Yes, came** or **No, did not come**. A no-show fades out in the calendar. If you charge no-show fees, Zenno tells you the fee; see [No-show fees](/money/no-show-fees/).

## 3 · Payment

Once the guest came, tap how they paid: **Cash**, **Card**, **Gift card** (enter the code and tap **Redeem**) or **Other / split…** for several payments. A green check appears on the card. Made a mistake? Tap **Undo**.

## More

Under **More** you find:

- **Move appointment**: pick a new date and one of the **Free times**. The guest gets an email with the new time and a new reminder.
- **Add no-show fee** or **Resolve fee**.
- **Invoice (PDF)**.
- **Reopen as confirmed**, if you closed a booking by mistake.
- **Cancel booking**: the guest is informed by email and the time becomes free again.

## Note for the team

Write a **Note for the team**, for example "wants Nann, strong pressure, back & neck", and tap **Save note**. Only your team sees it, never the guest. It also shows on the card in the calendar.
