**POS** is the first item in the menu. It opens the calendar, and below it your day's numbers (if you are allowed to see reports). Most of the day you only need this page.

## Day, week, month

At the top you switch between **Day**, **Week** and **Month**. Use the arrows to go back and forward and **Today** to jump back.

- **Day** shows one column per therapist. This is the best view at the counter.
- **Week** shows the whole week, all therapists together.
- **Month** gives you an overview; tap a day to open it.

## What the colours mean

Every therapist has their own colour. A booking card shows a small coloured dot, the start time, the guest's name and the treatment.

| You see | It means |
| --- | --- |
| Card in a therapist's colour | A confirmed booking |
| Yellow card with a dashed edge, "Please confirm" | The booking is waiting for your confirmation |
| Small green check | The booking is paid |
| Faded, crossed-out name, "Did not come" | The guest did not come (no-show) |
| Striped field | Blocked time, with its comment |
| Red line | The current time |

## Do something

- **Tap an empty time.** A small window asks *What would you like to do?*: **Add appointment** or **Block time**. While you move the mouse over the calendar, a small "+ 14:30" shows where you would start.
- **Tap a booking** to confirm it, enter whether the guest came, take the payment, move or cancel it, or write a note for the team.
- **Tap a blocked time** to change it, remove it, or turn it into a real appointment.
- The buttons **Sell voucher** and **Redeem voucher** sit at the top of the calendar.
- **Show therapists** lets you choose whose columns you see.

:::tip
On a tablet at the counter, keep the calendar on **Day**. Everything you need is one tap away.
:::
