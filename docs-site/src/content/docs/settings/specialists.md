Guests can only book when a therapist works. So working hours decide your free times.

## Add a therapist

1. Open **Specialists** → **Add Specialist**.
2. Enter the **Full Name** and, if you like, a short **Bio** and picture.
3. Tick the **Services Offered**.
4. Keep **Active (available for bookings)** on and tap **Create Specialist**.

## Working hours

On the therapist's card tap **Working Hours**, set from and to for each day (untick days off) and tap **Save Hours**.

## Time off

Tap **Time Blocks** for holidays or sick days: start, end and reason. For a short break today, [block time](/pos/block-time/) in the calendar instead.

## Shop closed days

Tap **Shop closed days** → **Add closed days** for public holidays or your own holiday. Nobody can book then, online or through the AI. Existing bookings stay.

## Can guests choose their therapist?

The switch **Customers choose their therapist** is on this page. When it is off, the booking page skips that step and gives the booking to a free therapist; the AI receptionist then does not name therapists either.
