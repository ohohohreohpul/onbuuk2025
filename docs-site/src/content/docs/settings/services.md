## Add a treatment

1. Open **Services** → **Add service**.
2. Enter the **Service name**, a **Description** and the **Category** (or **+ Create new category**).
3. Under **Durations & prices**, tap **Add duration** for each length, for example 60 min · 70 € and 90 min · 95 €.
4. Optional:
   - **Available for couples**: guests can book it for two.
   - **Hide from booking page**: you can still book it in the calendar, and vouchers stay valid.
   - **Buffer before / after (minutes)**: time to prepare or clean up.
   - **No-show fee**, **Late cancellation fee**, **Cancel before (hours)**.
5. Tap **Create service**.

Many treatments at once? Tap **Import**, download the template, fill it in and upload it.

## Add-on products

Under **Add-On Products** → **Add product** you offer extras that guests add to a booking, for example hot stones or an aroma oil: name, price, picture, and for which treatments it is available.
