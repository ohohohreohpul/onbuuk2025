Without a sender, no emails go out. Go to **Settings → Emails**.

## Booking emails

- **Confirm online bookings automatically**: off means you confirm each online booking yourself; the guest first gets a "request received" email.
- **Reminder the day before**: an email 24 hours before the appointment.
- **Email me about bookings**: you get an email for every new or cancelled online booking.

## Sender

Choose how emails are sent: **Resend** (recommended), SendGrid, Mailgun, or **SMTP** with your own mailbox (for example Strato or IONOS). For SMTP use the **SSL port** (465). Enter **From Email** and **From Name**, tap **Save Settings**, then **Send Test Email**.

The texts of the emails are under **Templates**; what was sent is under **Email Logs**.

:::note
The AI receptionist answers emails from the same mailbox. See [Turn it on and connect channels](/ai-receptionist/turn-on/).
:::
