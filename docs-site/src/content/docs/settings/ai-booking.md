This is different from the AI receptionist: here, **other** AI assistants book your free times for their users.

1. Open **AI booking** and switch on **Let AI assistants book appointments**.
2. **When an assistant books**: **Accept automatically**, or **Approve each booking myself** (you get an email to approve or decline).
3. Under **So assistants can find you**, choose your kind of business and your city.

You see **Bookings from assistants** and the **Activity** on the same page.
