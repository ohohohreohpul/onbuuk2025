---
title: Move from another tool
description: Bring customers, appointments and blocked times from EasyWeek, Shore, Treatwell and others.
sidebar:
  order: 6
---

1. Export your data from your old tool as CSV files.
2. In Zenno go to **Settings → Import** → **Import from another system**.
3. Choose what to import: **Customers**, **Appointments** or **Blocked times**, and upload the file.
4. Check the preview and import.

Imported appointments do not send confirmation emails, but guests still get the reminder the day before.

Treatments can be imported in **Services** → **Import**; vouchers in **Vouchers → Gift cards** → **Import CSV**.
