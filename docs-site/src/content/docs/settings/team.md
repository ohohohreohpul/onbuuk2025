1. Open **Team & Permissions** → **Invite Team Member**.
2. Enter the **Full name** and **Email address** and choose the **Primary role**.
3. Tap **Send invitation**. The person gets an email to create their login.

## Roles

| Role | Can |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Everything, including payments. |
| **Manager** | All bookings and calendars, customers, payments, vouchers, services, staff, reports. |
| **Receptionist** | Create, change and cancel all bookings, all calendars, customers and notes, take payments. |
| **Cashier** | See bookings and customers, take payments, see vouchers. |
| **Staff** | Their own bookings, calendar and availability; see customers and services. |

Give someone an extra role with **+ Add role**. **Deactivate** removes access without deleting the person. Open invitations can be resent or cancelled under **Invitations**.
