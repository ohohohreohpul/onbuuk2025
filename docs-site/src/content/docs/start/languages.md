**Your own language in the app:** tap **DE**, **EN** or **ไทย** at the bottom of the menu. Every person in your team can choose their own.

**The language your guests see** (booking page and emails) is set once for the shop in **Settings → Store Profile → Language of booking page and emails**.

The AI receptionist always answers in the guest's language.
