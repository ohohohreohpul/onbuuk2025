---
title: Use Zenno like an app
description: Put Zenno on the home screen of your tablet or phone.
sidebar:
  order: 3
---

Zenno runs in the browser, and you can add it to your home screen so it opens like an app, full screen and straight into your calendar.

- **iPad / iPhone (Safari):** open your Zenno address, tap the **Share** button, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android (Chrome):** open your Zenno address, tap the **⋮** menu, then **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
- **Computer (Chrome or Edge):** click the install icon at the right end of the address bar.

Zenno updates itself. When a new version comes out, it is loaded the next time you open it.
