title: First steps
description: Set up your shop in Zenno so guests can book you.

  order: 1
---

Do these steps once. Each takes a few minutes.

1. **Shop profile.** Go to **Settings → Store Profile**. Fill in **Business Name**, **Business Type**, **Phone Number**, **Business Address** and your **Permalink** (the address of your booking page). Choose the **Language of booking page and emails**. Tap **Save Profile**. Your booking page goes live when the profile is complete.
2. **Treatments.** Go to **Services** → **Add service**. Add each treatment with its **Durations & prices**. See [Services](/settings/services/).
3. **Therapists.** Go to **Specialists** → **Add Specialist**. Tick the **Services Offered** and set their **Working Hours**. See [Specialists](/settings/specialists/).
4. **Emails.** Go to **Settings → Emails** and set up a sender, so guests get confirmations and reminders. See [Emails](/settings/emails/).
5. **Payments (optional).** Go to **Settings → Payment** to accept card or PayPal online, or "Pay at venue". See [Payments](/money/payments/).
6. **Share your booking page.** Copy the link from **Store Profile** (**Copy**) and put it on your website, Instagram and Google profile.

:::tip[Coming from another booking tool?]
Under **Settings → Import** you can bring in customers, appointments and blocked times from EasyWeek, Shore, Treatwell and others (CSV files).
:::
