| Menu | What you do there |
| --- | --- |
| **POS** | Your calendar and the day's numbers. Add appointments, block time, sell and redeem vouchers. |
| **AI receptionist** | Chats from WhatsApp, email and your website; the receptionist's personality and channels. |
| **Bookings** | All bookings as a list, with what needs your action. |
| **Customers** | Every guest with visits, spending, no-shows and notes. |
| **Email marketing** | A monthly promotion email to your customers. |
| **Services** · **Add-On Products** | Treatments, durations, prices and extras. |
| **Specialists** | Therapists, their working hours and time off, shop closed days. |
| **Team & Permissions** | Invite your team and decide who sees what. |
| **AI booking** | Let ChatGPT, Siri and other AI assistants book you. |
| **No-Show Fees** · **Vouchers** · **Tax & Reports** | Money: fees, gift cards and passes, cash book and reports. |
| **Settings** | Shop profile, booking page look, emails, payments, invoices, import. |

You only see what your role allows. At the bottom of the menu: the language (**DE · EN · ไทย**), **Documentation**, **Get Help** and **Logout**.
